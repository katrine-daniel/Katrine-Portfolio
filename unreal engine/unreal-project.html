Classic Pixel Visuals & Kinematics FIG 01 · CORE ARCHITECTURE

This is my Unreal Engine 5 architecture study of Flappy Bird. Rather than a quick toy, it is engineered around production standards: non-linear falling gravity, pre-allocated actor pooling queues, and an inset collision capsule that prevents frustrating boundary clips.

In this rebuild, custom Blueprints calculate non-linear gravity falloff so the bird snaps briskly downwards after reaching its jump apex, while pre-allocated pipe actors cycle across a recycled viewport queue to maintain rock-solid 60 FPS performance.

02 / Engineering Insights

Small tuning changes matter.

01 / Hitbox Forgiveness

Making collision 15% smaller than the visible sprite prevents frustrating deaths when the player barely skims an edge, preserving momentum and rewarding risk.

02 / Memory & Actor Recycling

Reusing old pipe actors instead of repeatedly creating and destroying them prevents garbage collection frame drops, keeping gameplay butter-smooth.

03 / Gravity Snappiness

Tuning gravity stronger during the downward descent makes the game feel immediate and responsive rather than slow, mushy, or floaty.
